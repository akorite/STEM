# Surface Brief: Modern Pastel Biosphere Console

Scope: Biosphere Chamber Monitoring & Control Console
Mode: operate

## Direction contract

THESIS: Replaces cold, grim military-industrial cockpit aesthetic with a modern, delightfully cute pastel botanical console. Marries rigorous real-time agronomic telemetry (Tetens VPD, capacitive soil ADC, dual reservoir fluidics, closed-loop setpoints, 6-channel relays) with cheerful, inviting visual craft and an interactive plant companion.

OWN-WORLD: Soft cloud-porcelain canvas (#f8fafc), milky white pillowed cards with pastel candy hairlines, fresh mint and sakura rose accents, bouncy pill buttons, tactile pastel rocker toggles, cute test-tube reservoir vials, and an animated sprout companion celebrating optimal biosphere health.

STORY: Operator glances at the chamber and instantly feels calm and delighted: metrics are clear and friendly, the plant companion reflects growth comfort, and physical actuators respond with bouncy, tactile precision.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Responsive 100vh desktop dashboard. Top: Friendly pill navigation HUD. Row 1: 3 sensory pods (Atmosphere, Plant & Soil Companion, Fluidics). Row 2: Pastel vector strip-chart recorder with target bands + Tactile +/- setpoint steppers. Row 3: 6-channel pastel relay switchboard with flow valves + Clean live activity audit log.

FORM: Modern Pastel Botanical Sanctuary & Biosphere Console.

RISK: Avoiding pastel washed-out low contrast; guaranteed by deep slate typography (#0f172a / #334155), high-contrast badges (WCAG AA 4.5:1+), and crisp semantic outlines.
